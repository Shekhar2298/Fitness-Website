MAKE SPACE TO BREATHE

Quiet the noise.
Keep the good.

Meditation is not about emptying your mind. It is a practice of noticing what is here, then choosing where your attention goes next.

A softer kind
of strength.

A few intentional minutes can help you create space between a thought and your next response.

01 / FOCUS

Return to one thing

Use your breath, a sound, or a simple phrase as an anchor whenever attention wanders.

02 / AWARENESS

Notice without judgment

Thoughts and feelings can be present without needing to become instructions.

03 / REST

Reset your pace

Let your nervous system experience a slower rhythm before you step back into the day.

A five-minute
beginning.

Try this whenever you need a reset. Keep the practice easy enough to repeat tomorrow.

01 · Arrive

Sit comfortably, soften your shoulders, and feel the support beneath you.

02 · Breathe

Notice the natural movement of your breath without trying to force it.

03 · Return

When your mind wanders, gently come back to the next inhale and exhale.

04 · Close

Take one final breath and choose one kind intention for the next part of your day.

Give your mind a minute.

Start small, practice often, and let calm become something you can return to.
