FIND YOUR FLOW

Move with
more space.

Yoga connects breath, attention, and movement. Begin gently, listen closely, and let your practice meet you exactly where you are.

Why practice
yoga?

A regular practice can be a quiet reset for both body and mind. You do not need to be flexible to begin.

01 / BODY

More mobility

Move joints through comfortable ranges and build awareness of the muscles supporting you.

02 / BREATH

Less tension

Use slower breathing to soften effort and create a steadier response to busy moments.

03 / MIND

Clearer attention

Practice returning to one sensation, one breath, and one moment at a time.

Start with
simple poses.

Move slowly and stop before pain. A qualified teacher can help you adapt poses to your body.

Mountain pose

Stand tall with feet grounded, shoulders soft, and your breath easy.

Child’s pose

Rest your hips toward your heels and allow your spine to lengthen comfortably.

Cat-cow flow

Move gently between rounding and lengthening your spine with each breath.

Low lunge

Open the front of one hip while keeping the movement supported and controlled.

Take a breath. Begin.

Make a little room in your day for movement that leaves you feeling more at home in your body.
